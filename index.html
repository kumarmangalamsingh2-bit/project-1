    What to do in the first minutes

    A simple guideline that helps responders arrive faster. Built into the app as quick-access cards.

    Medical emergency
    1. Call 108 or 102 and give the exact location.
    2. Check breathing. Begin CPR if the person is unresponsive and not breathing normally.
    3. Do not move someone with a suspected head or spine injury.
    Fire
    1. Get everyone out and stay out. Call 101.
    2. Stay low under smoke and close doors behind you.
    3. Never use lifts.
    Crime or personal danger
    1. Move to a safe, public place if you can and call 100 or 112.
    2. Send your live location to your trusted contacts with SOS.
    Need blood urgently
    1. Tell the hospital the blood group and units required.
    2. Call your saved blood bank, then ask family and friends to donate.

    How E-Contact works

    1. Save
      Add trusted contacts and your local service numbers once.
    2. Tap
      Press SOS or pick a service. No menus to search in a crisis.
    3. Alert
      An SMS with your location goes to every saved contact.
    4. Connect
      One more tap calls the rescue team you need.

    Our goal is to set a clear guideline that helps Emergency Response Teams save time and lives, and to keep every piece of information you need within reach when it matters.
